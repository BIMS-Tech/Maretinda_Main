## How Maretinda charges sellers

Maretinda runs on a monthly or yearly subscription. There is no commission on
sales and no listing fees. Payment processing is deducted from online payments
before they are paid out.

This is the single most important thing to get right when answering a seller:
Maretinda does not take a percentage of sales. Any figure framed as a
"commission per sale" is wrong.

Every new seller's first month is free. Plans can be changed or cancelled at any
time. Paying yearly costs less than twelve months.

## Becoming a seller

Click "Sell on Maretinda" to open the application in the seller panel. It asks
for: the applicant's name, mobile and email; how the business is registered
(sole proprietor with DTI, corporation or partnership with SEC, cooperative with
CDA, and so on); the registered business name and the shop name buyers will see;
the business TIN and VAT status; the business address; what the shop will sell;
and agreement to the Seller Agreement and the privacy notice.

After sending the application the applicant gets an email with a link to
confirm their email address; the team approves an application only once it is
confirmed. Signing in to the seller panel before approval shows the
application's status, with "Send a new link" if the email is still unconfirmed.

The Maretinda team reviews the application, usually within a few business days,
and emails the decision. After approval the seller signs in, chooses a plan,
finishes the store setup (description, bank account for payouts, pickup address)
and uploads business documents for verification: for example the DTI or SEC
registration, the Mayor's permit, the BIR Certificate of Registration and a
valid ID. Products can be added straight away and go live once the documents are
approved; the shop then shows a "Verified" badge. Sellers of food, cosmetics or
health products are also asked for their FDA permits.

## Getting paid

Sellers are paid by bank transfer to the account in their store settings, 14
days after each order is delivered, so the buyer's 7-day return period has
passed. An open return holds that order until it is decided, and an approved
return comes off the amount. Cash on Delivery money is paid on the same rule
once the courier has sent it to Maretinda. The seller panel's "Payouts &
Invoices" page shows what is waiting, when it will be paid, and what was paid.
Payouts are not made to GCash or Maya.

## Shipping

Sellers ship their own orders. They can book Flying Tigers from the order page in
the seller panel (it currently serves selected provinces), and the order is then
marked shipped with its tracking number automatically. Sellers elsewhere can use
their own courier and add the tracking number to the order.

## Seller tools

The seller panel covers products, orders, shipping, vouchers, payouts, invoices
and analytics. What is available depends on the plan: product limits, staff
accounts, flash sales and promotions vary by plan.

Sellers get an email when a buyer reviews their shop or one of their products,
and when an item's stock drops to 5 or fewer and again when it runs out, so
they can restock before buyers are turned away.
