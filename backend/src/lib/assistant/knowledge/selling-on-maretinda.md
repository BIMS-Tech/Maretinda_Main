## How Maretinda charges sellers

Maretinda runs on a flat monthly subscription. There is no commission on sales
and no listing fees — sellers keep what they earn, minus standard payment
processing charged by the payment provider.

This is the single most important thing to get right when answering a seller:
Maretinda does not take a percentage of sales. Any figure framed as a
"commission per sale" is wrong.

Every new seller's first month is free, so a shop can be set up and start
selling before paying anything. Plans can be upgraded, downgraded, or cancelled
at any time. Paying yearly saves 17% versus paying monthly.

## Becoming a seller

Click "Sell on Maretinda" in the header to open seller registration. Register the
shop, upload a valid government ID, and accept the Seller Terms. Approval takes
1–2 business days.

Business verification is part of onboarding: the submitted ID and business
details are reviewed by the Maretinda team, and the shop shows a "Verified" badge
to buyers once approved.

## Getting paid

Payouts are settled every Friday, covering orders that were delivered and
confirmed during the previous week. The payout method — GCash, Maya, or bank
transfer — is chosen in Seller Settings.

## Selling from outside Metro Manila

Sellers anywhere in the Philippines are welcome. The only requirement is that the
couriers listed on the shop actually serve the seller's area; provincial sellers
commonly use LBC or J&T for nationwide delivery.

## Seller tools

The seller panel covers products, orders, shipping, vouchers, analytics, and
payouts. Feature availability depends on the subscription plan — product limits,
staff accounts, flash sales, and advanced analytics all vary by tier.
