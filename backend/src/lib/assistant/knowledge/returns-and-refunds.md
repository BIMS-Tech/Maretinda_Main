## Return window and eligibility

A buyer can ask for a return or refund within 7 days of the courier marking the
parcel delivered, for an item that arrived damaged or defective, is the wrong
item (product, size, colour or variation), is incomplete, is materially
different from the listing or counterfeit, or is food or a consumable past its
expiry. A parcel that was shipped but never arrived, or was lost in transit, can
also be claimed.

Not covered: a change of mind or ordering the wrong size, damage from misuse
after delivery, and opened personal care items, cosmetics, swimwear,
underwear, food, custom-made and digital goods, unless they arrived damaged,
expired or wrong. Items sent back must be unused, with all packaging, tags,
manuals and freebies. The full rules are at /returns-policy.

## How to request a return

My Account → Orders → select the order → "Request return". Choose the items,
how many, and the reason. Then add the proof: clear photos of the problem with
the shipping waybill on the parcel in view, and for a damaged, defective or
incomplete item an unedited unboxing video from the sealed parcel through to
checking the item. Enter a GCash, Maya or bank account (InstaPay) for the
refund. Only Maretinda's accounting team sees the account details.

## What happens next

1. The seller has 48 hours to answer: take the item back, refund without a
   return, or dispute the claim with proof. With no answer in 48 hours the
   request is approved automatically.
2. If the item goes back, the buyer sends it within 5 days of approval to the
   address shown on the return, then enters the courier and tracking number and
   what they paid for postage. For a valid claim the postage is added to the
   refund. A return not sent within 5 days is closed.
3. The seller checks the returned item within 48 hours of receiving it. Then
   the refund is sent.

If the seller disputes the claim, the buyer can press "Escalate to Maretinda"
on the return. Maretinda reviews the evidence from both sides and decides within
7 days; the decision is final within Maretinda, and the buyer may still contact
the DTI afterwards. Progress is under My Account → Returns, and the buyer is
emailed at each step.

## Refunds

Maretinda's accounting team sends every approved refund by hand to the account
the buyer gave: within 7–14 business days of approval for online payments, and
3–5 business days for cash on delivery. When it is sent, the buyer gets the
reference number, transfer date, exact amount and receipt by email, and it shows
on the order page.

How much: for a damaged, wrong, incomplete, not-as-described or lost item, 100%
of the item price, plus the original delivery fee when the whole order is
returned, plus the return postage the buyer paid. When a seller agrees to take
back an item that isn't covered, the buyer pays the delivery, the return postage
and the payment processing fee, which are taken off the refund.

## Cancelling and failed deliveries

Unpaid and cash-on-delivery orders can be cancelled from the order page until
the seller starts preparing them. A paid-online order can be cancelled before it
ships with "Request cancellation" on the order page; the refund is what was
paid less the payment processing fee (2.5% for now). If the seller or Maretinda
cancels a paid order, the buyer is refunded in full.

If a delivery fails because of a wrong address, an unreachable buyer or a
refused parcel, a prepaid order is refunded less the delivery fee, the courier's
return fee and the processing fee; a cash-on-delivery order needs no refund.
Buyers with repeated failed cash-on-delivery orders can no longer choose cash on
delivery.

## Damaged, wrong, or missing items

Photograph the item and its packaging with the waybill in view, and keep the
unboxing video, before throwing anything away. Then request a return from the
order page within 7 days of delivery and add them there.
