## Accepted payment methods

Checkout is powered by GiyaPay. Accepted methods:

- GCash
- Maya
- Visa and Mastercard, credit and debit
- InstaPay and QR Ph
- Cash on Delivery (COD)

Bank transfer is available for selected sellers only.

## Cash on Delivery

COD is available from most sellers in Metro Manila and major provincial cities.
Availability for a given address and seller is shown at checkout. COD orders
above ₱5,000 may require identity verification.

A COD order is not paid at checkout — it is collected in cash by the courier on
delivery, so the order shows as awaiting payment until then. That is expected and
does not mean the payment failed.

## Declined payments

Common causes: insufficient balance, a card limit, an incorrect OTP, or the
issuing bank blocking online transactions. Try a different payment method or
contact the bank. Nothing is charged when a payment is declined.

## Split payment

Split payment is not supported — one payment method per order. Placing two
separate orders is the current workaround.

## Currency and pricing

All prices are in Philippine pesos (₱) and include any seller-set taxes. The
total shown on the checkout review step is the final amount charged, including
shipping.

## Vouchers and discounts

Vouchers are applied on the cart or checkout page before payment. A voucher with
a minimum-order requirement only applies once the cart subtotal reaches that
amount; the subtotal it compares against excludes shipping.
