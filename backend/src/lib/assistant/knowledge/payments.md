## Accepted payment methods

Online payment goes through GiyaPay. The methods offered at checkout are:

- GCash
- Visa and Mastercard
- InstaPay

Cash on Delivery (COD) is offered where the seller supports it. The methods
available for a particular order are the ones shown at checkout. Maya, bank
transfer and instalments are not offered.

## Cash on Delivery

A COD order is not paid at checkout: the buyer pays the courier in cash when the
parcel arrives, so the order shows as awaiting payment until then. That is
expected and does not mean the payment failed.

## Declined payments

Common causes: insufficient balance, a card limit, an incorrect OTP, or the
issuing bank blocking online transactions. Try a different payment method or
contact the bank. Nothing is charged when a payment is declined.

## Paid but the order did not appear

If a buyer paid through GiyaPay and closed the page before returning to
Maretinda, the order may not show. They should write to support@maretinda.com
with the GiyaPay reference number; the support team can trace every hand-off to
GiyaPay.

## Split payment

Each order is paid with one method.

## Currency and pricing

All prices are in Philippine pesos (₱). The total shown on the checkout review
step is the amount charged, including shipping.

## Vouchers and discounts

Vouchers are applied on the cart or checkout page before payment. A voucher with
a minimum-order requirement only applies once the cart subtotal reaches that
amount; the subtotal it compares against excludes shipping.

## Invoices

Each order has an invoice issued in the seller's name. It is attached to the
order confirmation email and can be downloaded from the order page.
