## What Maretinda is

Maretinda is a Philippine online marketplace where independent sellers run their
own shops. Buyers browse and buy across many sellers in one place; each seller
handles their own inventory, pricing, and delivery.

Because it is a marketplace and not a single store, an order containing items
from two sellers becomes two shipments with two tracking numbers.

## Contacting support

Support is reachable at support@maretinda.com. The Help Center at /help-center
covers orders, shipping, returns, payments, account, and selling.

For anything specific to one order — a late parcel, a wrong item, a damaged
package — messaging the seller directly from the order page is faster than email,
because the seller holds the stock and arranges the courier.

## Messaging a seller

Every product and order page has a way to message the seller. Conversations live
under Messages in the account area. Sellers are expected to respond within
business hours.

## Browsing and finding products

Products are organised by category and can be filtered by price, seller, and
attributes. The Flash Sale section carries time-limited discounts, and Trending
shows what is selling now.
