## Delivery timeframes

Metro Manila: 1–3 business days.
Luzon provinces: 3–5 business days.
Visayas and Mindanao: 5–7 business days.

These are business days and exclude the day the order is placed. Peak seasons
(Christmas holidays, 11.11, 12.12) add delays across every courier.

## Couriers

Delivery is arranged by each individual seller, not centrally by Maretinda.
Most sellers use LBC, J&T Express, Ninja Van, or Lalamove for same-day delivery
within Metro Manila. The couriers a seller supports are shown before checkout.

Because the seller chooses the courier, two items bought from two sellers in one
session arrive as two separate parcels with separate tracking numbers.

## Shipping fees and free shipping

Shipping is charged per seller and calculated at checkout from the delivery
address. Many sellers offer free shipping above a minimum spend — product pages
carry a "Free Shipping" badge when that applies. Platform-wide free-shipping
promotions are announced in the Flash Sale section.

The delivery rate shown at checkout is the rate that gets charged; it does not
change after the order is placed.

## Changing a delivery address

An address can only be changed before the seller processes the shipment.
Message the seller as soon as possible through Messages, or contact support. Once
a tracking number exists the address is locked in with the courier and the order
has to be cancelled and re-placed instead.

## Nationwide coverage

Sellers ship across the Philippines. Provincial sellers typically use LBC or
J&T for nationwide reach. Availability for a specific address is confirmed at
checkout — if no courier serves the area, checkout blocks before payment.
