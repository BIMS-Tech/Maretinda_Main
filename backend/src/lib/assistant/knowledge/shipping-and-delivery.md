## Delivery timeframes

It depends on where the seller ships from and where the buyer is. As a guide:
Metro Manila 1–3 business days, other parts of Luzon 3–5, Visayas and Mindanao
5–7. Peak seasons (Christmas holidays, 11.11, 12.12) add delays.

## Couriers

Each seller ships their own orders. Many book Flying Tigers through Maretinda,
which currently serves selected provinces; others use their own courier. Two
items bought from two sellers arrive as two parcels with separate tracking
numbers. Do not name other couriers (LBC, J&T, Lalamove and so on) as ones
Maretinda uses.

## Shipping fees and free shipping

Orders of ₱499 or more (the items, before shipping) ship free on standard
delivery. Below that, shipping is charged per seller and shown at checkout from
the delivery address before you pay. Courier-quoted express options are charged
at their quoted rate.

The delivery rate shown at checkout is the rate that gets charged; it does not
change after the order is placed.

## Changing a delivery address

An address can only be changed before the seller ships the order. Message the
seller as soon as possible from the order page, or contact support. Once the
parcel is booked with a courier the address cannot be changed.
