# Release 26 November 2026

Goes to test (`main`) and production (`production`) together. Covers everything on `main` since the 19 November production release.

## In short

- **Sellers signing in on the storefront.** A seller's email and password used to leave them half signed in and bounced back to an empty form. Now the storefront says it's a shop's account, and offers to start shopping with the same sign-in or to open the Seller Centre. The mobile app does the same. The admin and seller panels now explain a sign-in from the wrong kind of account instead of bouncing it.
- **Search, sorting and filters that work.** Sorting never worked: Algolia's sort indexes were never created. Listings now run on Maretinda's own search: typo-tolerant, understands Filipino words, 8 sorts, and filters for brand, colour, size, price, rating, on sale and in stock. Algolia is no longer used by the storefront.
- **A checkout that couldn't move on.** In production builds, "Continue to payment", cart changes and filters could hang for good after the page already had its answer. Fixed (see "For developers").
- **Motion across the platform.** Headlines whose words rise in, sections arriving one after another, a product card that grows into its page, items flying into the cart, sliding selection pills and numbers that count up. It covers the storefront, both panels, the seller website and the app, and is switched off for anyone whose device asks for less motion.
- **How sellers get paid, step by step.** Payouts & Invoices in the seller panel now shows the GiyaPay flow: paid online → delivered → 14-day hold → next bank file (TAMA for Metrobank, DFT for other banks) → in your bank. Cash on delivery has its own three steps. Each step shows how much of the shop's money is there now, and the page warns when bank details are missing.
- **Delivery setup, stock and invoices for sellers** (from the returns, invoices and seller guides work): guided delivery setup, a one-page stock table, step-by-step how-tos, and documents in the BIR e-invoice template layout.

## Buyers (storefront and app)

- New search page `/search` with suggestions while typing, "did you mean", filter chips, and a filter sheet on phones. Old `/categories?query=` links redirect there.
- Categories, collections and seller pages share the same product cards, with quick add for single-variant products.
- Picking size XL could put size L in the cart. It now matches the exact options.
- Cart drawer; quantities change at once and settle when the shop confirms.
- Each shop's delivery choices are shown as radio buttons at checkout. Opening checkout with an empty cart goes to the cart instead of a "Page not found".
- Sign-up showed a password as valid that the form then refused. There is now one password rule everywhere: 8+ characters, an uppercase and a lowercase letter, and a number or symbol.
- Password reset no longer says it succeeded when it didn't.
- Account pages restyled; the wishlist removes at once, with Undo.
- Home page first load is down from 516 kB to 187 kB.
- New public page `/verify-invoice/[id]` for the QR code on invoices, without the buyer's details.

**Mobile app** (in the repository; not yet published to the stores):
- Prices were shown at a hundredth of their value.
- Search, sorts and filters now use the same search as the website.
- Seller sign-in works as on the storefront.
- The wishlist and shop pages called routes that don't exist.
- Brand colours, heading font and app icons drawn from the logo mark. The icons were plain purple squares.

## Sellers

- Payouts & Invoices: "How you get paid" steps as described above. Recent Payouts on the dashboard reads the same GiyaPay ledger.
- The dashboard's Recent Orders showed a hundredth of each total. The chart drew in colours its legend didn't use.
- Shipping → Set up delivery does every step in one go: pickup place, delivery fees, area, free from ₱499, return option. A product is published only once buyers can check out with it.
- Inventory and each product page have a plain stock table. Stock can't go below what is sold and waiting to ship.
- Low-stock emails go to Growth and Premium shops, not Starter.
- Documents: until the BIR Permit to Issue is set, Maretinda's documents are **billing statements** and sellers' order documents are **order summaries**. Each says it is not an invoice. A monthly document lists the fees taken from payouts.
- Signing in with a shopper's password says the sign-in has no shop. It used to say "your shop is on its way".

## Admin

- A seller's or shopper's password at the admin sign-in says it isn't an admin's.
- Store front settings no longer ask for a typed-in rating count and "sold this week": they come from real reviews and orders.
- Invoices: credit notes from Admin → Invoices → Credit, and a structured data download for the BIR. Issued documents can't be edited.

## Deploying

1. **Backend first.** The new storefront calls `/store/search`, `/store/auth/shopper-profile` and `/store/invoice-verify/:id`. In `deploy-do.yml` the store job now waits for the backend job.
2. **Migration** `1771000000000_einvoice_fields` runs on release (custom migrations, `release.sh`). It adds invoice columns, `merchant_number`, and a trigger that refuses UPDATE/DELETE on issued invoices. `deploy/scripts/scrub-test-data.sql` disables that trigger to clear invoices on the test copy.
3. **Production `backend.env`:** if it has `INVOICE_ISSUER_VAT_REGISTERED=false`, remove that line: the VAT status now defaults to BIMS's COR. The new optional settings `INVOICE_PTI_NUMBER` and `SELLER_INVOICING_ENABLED` stay empty until the BIR permit and the RDO answer arrive. `SEARCH_FRESH_MS` (default 15 s) sets how fresh search results are.
4. The store build no longer needs Algolia; its build arguments are still accepted and ignored.
5. Nothing to do for the mobile app until it is published. `EXPO_PUBLIC_SELLER_URL` is the optional new setting.

## For developers

- **React backport.** Next 15.5 bundles a React that drops a Suspense "ping" arriving mid-render, so a same-page navigation could hang with its answer already loaded. `maretinda-store/scripts/react-lost-ping-loader.cjs` (wired in `next.config.ts`, webpack builds only) applies the one-line fix React ships in Next 16. A test fails the build if the bundled React changes. Remove the loader, its `next.config.ts` entry and the test after upgrading to Next 16.
- Search lives in `backend/src/lib/search/`. Sign-in for other accounts lives in `backend/src/lib/shopper-profile.ts`.
- Motion helpers: storefront `src/components/motion/`; both panels `src/components/common/motion/motion.tsx` and `src/brand-theme.css` (keep the two copies the same); app `src/components/motion/`; website `Masked.tsx` and `globals.css`.
- Removed: the seller panel's unrouted Stripe-style Payouts page, and an accidentally committed `node_modules` symlink in `backend/src/modules/payment-giyapay` that stopped Medusa from starting in the main checkout.
